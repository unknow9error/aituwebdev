Coffee and Warm Drinks

Espresso Drinks · Drink Prices

We prepare classic espresso, filter coffee and signature teas every day. Milk alternatives such as oat or almond milk cost an extra 300 KZT. You can ask the barista for less sweet syrups or decaf options.

Popular Drinks

Spanish Latte

A double shot of espresso combined with steamed milk and condensed milk. Our guests call it the sweet fuel for cold mornings.

Raf Coffee

Espresso whipped together with cream and vanilla sugar into a thick foam. It originated in Moscow and remains a local favorite in Astana.

Sea Buckthorn Tea

Warm berry tea brewed with fresh sea buckthorn, honey and orange slices.
