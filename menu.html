Breakfast with your coffee

Breakfast options · Compare ingredients

I chose three dishes from an earlier Golden Coffee menu. Check current availability and prices with the cafe. Compare the ingredients and ask about any products you need to avoid.

Three breakfast options

Golden Breakfast

French baguette is served with eggs and salmon. The ingredient list also mentions salad mix, meaning salad greens.

Chef's Breakfast

Eggs and salmon come with avocado, herbs, salad, toast and kurt. This breakfast has different bread and more extras than Golden Breakfast.

Paradise Porridge

Oatmeal with milk, dates, raisins and pistachios is the sweet breakfast option.
