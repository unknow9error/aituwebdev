Golden Coffee

Coffee shop · Astana

Coffee and breakfast on Uly Dala

Golden Coffee is a coffee shop at 65/2 Uly Dala Avenue in Astana. I collected the address, opening hours, phone number and a few breakfast dishes on this website. The contact details belong to this branch.

The menu page lists three breakfasts: baguette with salmon, eggs with avocado, and oatmeal with dried fruit. Their descriptions make it easier to compare ingredients. Check availability and prices with the cafe before ordering.

Before your visit

The visit page has a map link and the cafe's phone number. You can check the location and plan your route. Call ahead if you need to ask about breakfast ingredients or holiday opening hours.

You can also share the address by email. The link opens a new message, and you choose who to send it to.

Contact the cafe by phone, WhatsApp or Instagram. The visit form lets you enter a date, number of guests, contact details and any preferences for your visit.
